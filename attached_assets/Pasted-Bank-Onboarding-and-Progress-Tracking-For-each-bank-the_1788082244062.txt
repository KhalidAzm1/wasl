Bank Onboarding and Progress Tracking
For each bank, the platform shall provide two main tracks:
Technical Track
The Technical Track shall consist of five sequential phases:
Kick-Off
Analysis
Development and Integration
Testing
Rollout
Commercial and Agreements Track
The Commercial and Agreements Track shall include the following default activities:
Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
Commercial Agreement
The platform shall also provide an “Add Agreement Activity” button, allowing authorized users to create and add additional agreement activities based on the bank’s requirements.
 
Bank and Product Roadmap Dashboard
The platform shall provide a centralized roadmap dashboard displaying all onboarded banks and their associated products.
For each bank, the dashboard shall:
Display the bank’s name.
List all associated products, such as E1, E2, and S3.
Present a separate roadmap for each product across the following phases:
Kick-Off
Analysis
Development and Integration
Testing
Rollout
Display the planned timeframe for each phase and the overall product.
Show the product’s current phase and the number of days spent in that phase.
Automatically highlight products that exceed the planned phase duration as delayed or stuck.
Display the number of delay days against the planned timeline.
Example: Wachovia Bank – E1 – Analysis – 40 days in the current phase – Delayed by 10 days.
 
Daily Update Monitoring and Bank Ownership
Each bank shall be assigned a designated Project Manager or Account Manager.
The system shall allow administrators to define and update the manager assigned to each bank.
A daily progress update shall be required for every bank and its associated products.
If no update is submitted by the configured daily cut-off time, the system shall automatically notify:
Abdelrahman Alyousef
Ziyad Alnaimi
Majid Alomran
Ali Alslman 
The notification shall identify the bank, its assigned manager, the affected products, and the date of the latest submitted update.
The bank-to-manager assignments shall be configured once the Project Manager and Account Manager list is provided.
 
Microsoft Teams Integration
The platform shall integrate with Microsoft Teams to allow authorized users to submit progress updates through Teams messages.
Project Managers shall be able to update the Technical Track for their assigned banks and products, including:
Kick-Off
Analysis
Development and Integration
Testing
Rollout
Authorized Legal Team members shall be able to update the Commercial and Agreements Track, including the NDA, Commercial Agreement, and any additional agreement activities.
The system shall validate the sender’s identity and permissions before applying any update.
After processing the message, the system shall send a confirmation or failure notification to the sender through Teams.
The required message content, format, and update commands shall be defined at a later stage.
 
CEO Requests
Each bank shall have a dedicated section titled “CEO Requests.”
This section shall allow authorized Project Managers and Legal Team members to submit requests requiring the CEO’s support, direction, or intervention.
Each request shall include:
Bank and associated product
Request title and description
Required action
Requester’s name and department
Priority
Submission date
Current status
Examples may include requesting senior management intervention to engage the other party and accelerate the finalization of an agreement.
The system shall maintain a history of all requests and their status updates for each bank.
 
Stage-Level Progress Updates
Each phase within the Technical Track shall contain a dedicated “Updates” section:
Kick-Off
Analysis
Development and Integration
Testing
Rollout
The assigned Project Manager shall be able to submit progress updates under the relevant phase for each bank and product.
Each update shall record:
Update details
Submitted by
Submission date and time
Related bank, product, and phase
Updates may be submitted directly through the platform or through the Microsoft Teams integration.
The system shall maintain a chronological update history and display the most recent update on the roadmap dashboard.
Stage-level updates shall be considered when determining whether the required daily update has been submitted.
